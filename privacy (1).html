Privacy Policy — Sudaul

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data Storage (100% Local on Device)

Sudaul stores all your workout progress, name, weight logs, water intake, and calorie logs locally on your device using browser LocalStorage. We do not collect, upload, or sell any personal data to external servers.

2. Permissions

Sudaul does not require camera, microphone, location, or contacts permissions. Screen WakeLock is used only during active workouts to keep the timer visible.

3. Health Disclaimer

Sudaul provides general fitness and vegetarian nutrition guidance. Always consult a qualified physician before starting any exercise or diet program.

4. Data Deletion

You can erase all your data anytime inside the app by going to Settings → "Start over (erase all data)".
